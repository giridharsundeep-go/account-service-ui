🛑 Common Pitfalls to Avoid

  • Weaponizing Velocity: Avoid comparing velocity across different teams; velocity is relative to team estimation.
  • Vanity Metrics: Focus on business outcomes over raw task counts.
  • Over-measuring: Measure actionable KPIs rather than tracking data that creates multitasking bottlenecks.
